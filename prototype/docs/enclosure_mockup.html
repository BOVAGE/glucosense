Assembly Helper

1
Vero Board Prep
Solder female headers & male I2C pin rows onto the Vero board. Add 4.7kΩ I2C pull-ups.
2
Lid Component Mounting
Cut the OLED window. Drill button holes. Screw buttons and glue the OLED to the lid.
3
Mount Standoff Spacers
Secure four 15mm standoffs (spacers) to the top lid using M3 flat-head screws.
4
Side Wall Customizations
Cut the side USB slot. Mount the MAX30100 sensor board vertically behind the thumb tunnel hole.
5
Vero Board Attachment & Wire
Mount the Vero Board upside down on standoffs. Plug in all DuPont wire harnesses to ESP32.
